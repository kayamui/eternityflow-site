How It Works

01

Automated Data Pipeline

Zero manual preprocessing

Select any location on the globe. Eternity Flow automatically assembles the terrain, land cover, soil and rainfall statistics for that exact place — fetched, aligned and conditioned without a single local file. Even sharper with your own city-scale terrain.

02

Real Physics, GPU Speed

2D shallow-water equations

GPU-accelerated 2D shallow-water solvers with mass conservation checked on every run. Buildings steer street flow, storm-drain networks can be coupled, and antecedent soil moisture comes from data — not assumptions.

SolverACC + SGC / full SWE (HLLC)
ComputeJAX + CUDA, multi-GPU
BuildingsResistance / burn / obstacle
Mass balance< 0.01% closure

İstanbul Case Study

The Model vs. The News

We fed the model the rainfall that actually fell — 204 mm over 60 hours — on the city's 5 m terrain, then compared the result with the locations the news reported underwater. The sites were collected from news reports first and geocoded independently; the model was sampled afterwards.

Forecast In

Operational weather-model rainfall (e.g. WRF) is ingested automatically, in space and time.

The same engine that reproduced İstanbul turns the forecast into street-scale depth and hazard, hours ahead of the event.
