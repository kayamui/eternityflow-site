One platform, anywhere on Earth

Pick a point on the map. Eternity Flow assembles the terrain, soil, land cover, buildings and rainfall statistics for that exact place — then solves the physics.

Global & automatic

Worldwide elevation, soils, land cover and multi-source rainfall statistics are fetched and fused automatically. Works from Rize to Rotterdam without a single local file — and gets even sharper with your own 5 m city terrain.

Real physics, GPU speed

2D shallow-water equations on the GPU, mass-conservation checked on every run. Buildings steer street flow, storm drains can be coupled, antecedent soil moisture comes from data — not assumptions.

Decision-ready output

Interactive 3D replay with per-street depth, velocity and hazard; per-building damage in €; displaced-population estimates; branded PDF reports; a single offline HTML file you can hand to anyone.

From forecast to street-level warning

The same engine that reproduced İstanbul runs on weather forecasts: feed it a numerical forecast and it answers the question that matters — which street, how deep, and at what hour. Flood intelligence before the first drop, not after the damage.

1 · Forecast in

Operational weather-model rainfall (e.g. WRF) is ingested automatically, in space and time.

2 · Physics runs

The GPU solver turns the forecast into street-scale depth and hazard, hours ahead of the event.

3 · People warned

Neighbourhood-level alerts with depth, timing and hazard class — ready for civil protection workflows.
